How Far : Golf Support

How Far : Golf provides camera-based augmented reality and GPS measurement tools for approach shots, putting, and longer distances.

Contact

For questions, bug reports, or feedback, contact: lhm4802@nate.com

When reporting a problem, please include:

  • Your iPhone model
  • Your iOS version
  • The app version
  • A description of the problem

Measurement accuracy

Camera and GPS measurements are estimates. Accuracy may vary depending on lighting, surface detection, device sensors, GPS reception, and the surrounding environment. The app is not a certified surveying instrument.

Permissions

Camera access is required for Approach and Putting measurements. Location access is required for GPS-based Longrange measurement.
