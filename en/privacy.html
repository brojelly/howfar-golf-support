How Far : Golf Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how How Far : Golf handles information when you use the app.

Information we collect

How Far : Golf does not require an account and does not operate a server that collects or stores your personal information.

Camera and augmented reality

The app uses the camera and augmented reality features to estimate distances in Approach and Putting modes. Camera images are processed on your device for measurement purposes. The app does not save or upload photos or video recordings.

Location and compass

The app uses your location, heading, and compass information to display your position and measure straight-line distances in Longrange mode. This information is used while you are using the app.

Map content is provided through Apple MapKit. Location and map requests may be processed by Apple according to Apple's privacy policy.

App preferences

Preferences such as language and measurement unit may be stored locally on your device. These preferences are not transmitted to our servers.

Analytics and advertising

How Far : Golf does not include third-party advertising or third-party analytics services.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The effective date shown above will be updated when changes are made.
